  3. Watch the sunrise after staying up together.
  4. Watch a movie together alone.
  5. Wear matching outfits and go out.
  6. Recreate our very first walk + first kiss.
  7. Bake something together.
  8. Late night maccas runs.
  9. Cuddle in a bed together.
  10. Watch the stars together away from here.
  11. Write each other little notes and swap them on the same day.
  12. Have one no-phone date where it's just us and the moment.
  13. Try something new together just for the memory of it.
  14. Celebrate our own holidays eg. Throwback Tuesday.
  15. Go on a cruise together and have the time of our lives.
  16. Spend a whole day (24 hours) doing absolutely nothing but being together.
  17. Keep learning every version of you as you grow.
  18. Be there for all the birthdays, bad days, victories, quiet nights, and everything in between.
And if I get one extra thing beyond the list, I just want a life where loving you never becomes something I have to look back on.
